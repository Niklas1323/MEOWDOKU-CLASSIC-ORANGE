## Full description (max. 4000 characters)

```
Welcome to MEOWDOKI, the cozy logic puzzle with cats!

The rules are simple: place exactly one cat in every row, every column and every color area. Cats need their space, so they can never touch, not even diagonally. Sounds easy? The bigger the board, the trickier it gets.

You never have to guess. Mark the tiles that can't hold a cat, and there is always a tile that must be one. Every level starts with a little helper: a cat that is already in place, or a color area that is only one tile big.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap a tile to mark it with ✕
• Double-tap a tile to place a cat
• A short tutorial shows you everything step by step
• No timer: play at your own pace

100 LEVELS
• From 5×5 up to 10×10
• Four difficulties: Easy, Medium, Hard and Expert
• Earn up to 3 stars per level: no mistakes and no hints for the full score

A NEW DAILY CHALLENGE EVERY DAY
• A fresh puzzle waiting for you every day
• Daily Challenges give you 50% more XP
• Missed a day? Play past Daily Challenges from the calendar

KEEP YOUR STREAK PURRING
• Solve 3 puzzles every day to keep your streak going
• Levels and the Daily Challenge both count
• See at a glance how many days this week you reached your daily goal

HEARTS AND HINTS
• A wrong cat costs one heart, so place your cats with care
• Solve levels to win hearts back
• Stuck? A hint places a correct cat for you

XP AND SKINS
• Earn XP for every solved puzzle, with a bonus for clean solves
• Unlock 14 cats, from the Orange Tabby and the Siamese to the Golden Cat
• Style your puzzle with 10 boards like Pastel, Sunset, Ocean and Forest

COZY FROM START TO FINISH
• Lovingly illustrated scenes with sleepy cats
• Calm lo-fi music, sound effects and vibration, each one can be turned off
• Perfect for a few relaxing puzzles before bed
• In English and German

FREE TO PLAY
MEOWDOKI is free and supported by ads. Most ads are optional and you choose to watch them, for example to double your rewards, refill your hearts, get an extra hint or unlock the next cat or board for free. A short full-screen ad may appear after you finish a level. There are no ads before a level starts. There are no purchases with real money.

No account needed: your progress is saved only on your device.

Grab a cup of tea, get cozy and help the cats find their spot. Meow!
```
